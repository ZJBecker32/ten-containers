## Cilantro Lime Taco Bowls

**Serves:** 10
**Prep Time:** 20 min
**Cook Time:** 55 min

**Ingredients:**

  - 5 lbs ground beef (90/10) or ground turkey
  - 4 packets taco seasoning
  - 3 cans black beans
  - 3 ½ cups jasmine rice (dry)
  - 2–3 limes, juiced (or 3–4 tbsp bottled)
  - ½ bunch fresh cilantro, chopped (optional)
  - 6 bell peppers
  - 1 large onion
  - 1 bag frozen sweet corn (12–16 oz)
  - Olive oil, as needed
  - Shredded cheese, salsa, sour cream (added fresh when eating)

**Steps:**

1.  Rinse 3 ½ cups jasmine rice 2–3 times until the water runs mostly clear. Add to the Instant Pot with 3 ½ cups water (1:1).

2.  Confirm the valve is set to SEALING and the float valve pops up. Pressure Cook (Manual) on High for 3 minutes, natural release 10 minutes, then quick release.

3.  Fluff the rice, then stir in the lime juice and chopped cilantro. Leave the lid off to cool. Add the lime after cooking, not before.

4.  Heat a large pan over medium-high. Add 5 lbs ground beef, breaking it up as it cooks. Brown 10–12 minutes until no pink remains and it reaches 165°F. Work in 2 batches if the pan is crowded — crowding steams the meat instead of browning it.

5.  Drain the excess fat thoroughly. Beef renders noticeably more than turkey, and skipping this makes the bowls greasy by day 3.

6.  Add 4 packets taco seasoning plus the water called for on the packets. Simmer 2–3 minutes until thickened.

7.  In a separate pan, heat a little olive oil. Add the diced bell peppers and onion and sauté 6–8 minutes until softened.

8.  Add the frozen corn for the last 3–4 minutes. Let it pick up a few golden char spots for a fire-roasted flavor.

9.  Rinse and drain 3 cans black beans, then warm them in a small pot or the microwave. Rinsing cuts sodium and stops the cloudy can liquid from making the bowls mushy.

10. Let the meat, rice, veggies, and beans cool 10 minutes before portioning. Sealing hot food causes condensation and sogginess.

11. Assembly line across 10 open containers: 170 g meat, then 150 g rice, then 80 g beans, then 110 g veg and corn. No separate sauce container — the taco seasoning is already in the meat.

12. Label with a dry-erase marker. 6 containers to the fridge for the first half of the week, 4 to the freezer. Move freezer containers to the fridge the night before eating.

**Notes:**

Lessons from real cook sessions:

Protein was bumped from 3.5 lbs to 5 lbs — 120 g per container was too light to stay full through the afternoon. Bell peppers shrink hard when cooked; 3 peppers only yielded ~170 g total, so buy 6. Rice at 3 ½ cups dry yields ~1500 g cooked, which lands 150 g per container.

Aldi is frequently out of fresh limes and cilantro. Bottled lime juice is a fine fallback and the dish still works with the cilantro skipped entirely.

Double-check the Instant Pot valve is on SEALING before walking away. This has gone wrong before.

Cold toppings — cheese, salsa, sour cream — go on fresh at eating time, never packed into the containers.

This is the cheapest prep in the rotation and the only one requiring no oven.

Open item: sodium runs high at ~950 mg per container, most of it from the 4 seasoning packets. Next version, try swapping 2 packets for bulk cumin, chili powder, paprika, garlic powder, and oregano.
