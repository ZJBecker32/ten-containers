## Honey Garlic Crockpot Shredded Chicken

**Serves:** 10
**Prep Time:** 15 min
**Cook Time:** 3–4 hrs

**Ingredients:**

  - 6 lbs chicken breast, whole (not cubed)
  - ½ cup chicken broth
  - ⅔ cup honey
  - ½ cup low-sodium soy sauce
  - 8–9 cloves garlic, minced
  - 4 tbsp butter
  - 1 ½ tbsp cornstarch + 3 tbsp cold water
  - ½ cup reserved crockpot liquid
  - 3 ½ cups jasmine rice (dry)
  - ~2 lbs fresh green beans
  - Olive oil, salt, pepper

**Steps:**

1.  Place 6 lbs whole chicken breasts in the crockpot. Season with salt and pepper and add ½ cup chicken broth for moisture. No cutting required — this is the point of the recipe.

2.  Cook on High 3–4 hours, or Low 6–7 hours, until the chicken reaches 165°F and is fork-tender.

3.  Drain the cooking liquid but reserve ½ cup of it. Draining prevents a watery result; the reserved half cup goes back into the sauce for volume and flavor without added sugar.

4.  Shred the chicken with a hand mixer directly in the crockpot — 6 lbs takes about 30 seconds. A few pulses is enough; over-mixing turns it mushy.

5.  In a saucepan, combine ⅔ cup honey, ½ cup low-sodium soy sauce, 8–9 cloves minced garlic, 4 tbsp butter, and the ½ cup reserved crockpot liquid. Simmer 3–4 minutes.

6.  Taste the sauce before thickening. Low-sodium soy is noticeably less punchy than regular — add a splash more soy or a pinch of salt if it reads flat.

7.  Whisk 1 ½ tbsp cornstarch into 3 tbsp cold water, then stir the slurry into the simmering sauce. Cook 1–2 minutes until it coats the back of a spoon.

8.  Pour the sauce over the shredded chicken and stir. Hold on Warm for 10–15 minutes so the shredded meat absorbs it.

9.  During the last ~20 minutes of chicken time, rinse 3 ½ cups jasmine rice until the water runs clear. Add to the Instant Pot with 3 ½ cups water. Valve on SEALING. Pressure Cook High 3 minutes, natural release 10 minutes, quick release. Fluff and cool.

10. Trim the green beans and toss with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Air roast at 400°F in the Ninja toaster oven for 15–18 minutes until tender with slightly crispy tips.

11. Cool everything 10–15 minutes. Assembly line across 10 containers: 170 g chicken, then 140 g rice, then 90 g green beans. The sauce is already in the chicken, so no separate sauce container.

12. Label with a dry-erase marker. 6 to the fridge, 4 to the freezer. Move freezer containers to the fridge the night before.

**Notes:**

RETIRED after the 2026-08-09 cook. The sauce is too sweet — 17 g of added
sugar per container, over the ceiling, and it read as a glaze rather than a
savory sauce. Kept here because the numbers below are observed and because the
chicken underneath was the best pulled chicken the rotation has produced.

Two of those corrected `docs/standing-parameters.md`. Green beans had been
recorded at an implied 0.99, so this batch came up 355 g short — 54 g of
vegetable per container against a 90 g plan. For 900 g roasted, buy 3 ⅓ lbs.
Chicken came in at 0.637, below the 0.70–0.76 the file had recorded.

Portioned as 7 fridge containers with all the beans at 78 g each, and 3
freezer containers with none, on the reasoning that thin roasted green beans
are the worst thing in the container to freeze and fresh vegetable can be
added after thawing.

What to keep: whole breasts in the crockpot, shredded with a hand mixer.
What to replace: the honey. A soy-and-aromatics sauce with a couple of
tablespoons of sugar lands near 3 g per container instead of 17.

Sauce was scaled up from a smaller scratch recipe. Shredded chicken absorbs
far more sauce than cubed does, and the original ½ cup honey / ⅓ cup soy
version yields roughly 300 g of sauce against 6 lbs of meat — not enough.
The reserved crockpot liquid is what closes the volume gap without adding
sugar. If it still reads dry after step 8, add more reserved liquid rather
than more honey.

Low-sodium soy was chosen deliberately: Aldi does not reliably carry a
bottled honey garlic sauce, and the bottled version runs ~25 g added sugar
and ~1400 mg sodium per container. The scratch version should land closer
to 17 g and 700 mg.

Crockpot whole breasts retain considerably more weight than oven-cubed
chicken, and eliminate the raw chicken cutting step entirely. Trade-off is
no seared flavor — the sauce carries it instead.

Green beans are more forgiving than broccoli and hard to overcook.

The beans go in the Ninja toaster oven at 400°F air roast rather than the main
oven at 425°F — the fan makes up the 25°F, so the timing is unchanged. Nothing
in this recipe needs the main oven, which leaves it free for a second prep.
