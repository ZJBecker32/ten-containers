## What happened

Second cook, and the one that closed the recipe. Chicken bought as 2.89 +
2.58 lbs because the store had no good sizes — 5.47 lbs against a 5 ¾ lb
target, about 5% under, which landed 187 g of sauced chicken per container
against a 190 g target. No adjustment needed.

The weigh step added in v2 did its job: 1420 g of shredded chicken, unsauced,
off 2481 g of package weight. **0.572 on a package basis**, against the 0.65
this repo had been planning at.

Two explanations are confounded in that figure. The breasts were visibly
trimmed before cooking, and the cook was carried to shreddability near 200°F
rather than stopped at 165°F. Both remove weight, and this data cannot
separate them. Package basis is the useful one for shopping regardless, since
package weight is what gets bought.

It also retroactively graded the previous session's discarded number. That
cook implied 0.565 after estimating the sauce at 454 g from its ingredients;
0.572 puts the real sauce near 434 g, so the estimate was 4% out. The right
call was still to discard it — a lucky estimate and a measurement are not the
same thing, and nothing about the arithmetic at the time said which it would
turn out to be.

Rice and broccoli were not re-measured this session; the figures in the recipe
come from 2026-09-13.
