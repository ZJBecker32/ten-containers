## What happened

Chicken bought as two packs, 3.47 + 2.76 lbs. Times were not recorded.

Green beans came up 355 g short against the 900 g plan — the recipe was built
on a standing-parameters figure that implied a 0.99 yield, and the real one is
0.601. Portioned around it rather than spreading the shortfall thin: all
545 g went into the 7 containers being eaten this week at 78 g each, and the
3 freezer containers were packed with no green beans at all. Thin roasted
green beans are the worst component in the container to freeze, and fresh
vegetable can be added after thawing.

Chicken measured 0.637, below the 0.70–0.76 range the file had recorded.
Rice came in high at 457 g per dry cup, above the previous 407–428.

The pulled chicken itself was the best the rotation has produced — whole
breasts in the crockpot, shredded with a hand mixer, is settled as the method.

The sauce was the failure. Too sweet, reading as a glaze rather than a savory
sauce, consistent with its 17 g of added sugar per container being over the
ceiling. Recipe retired on that basis; the technique and the yields carry
forward.
