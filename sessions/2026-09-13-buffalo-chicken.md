## What happened

First cook of this recipe. Portioned 10 containers at 191 g sauced chicken,
158 g rice, 95 g broccoli — roughly 570 kcal and 51 g of protein, both inside
their bands.

Rice and broccoli measured cleanly. The chicken did not, for two reasons that
were both the recipe's fault rather than the cook's.

It was weighed after the sauce was stirred through, at 1914 g, and v1 gave no
instruction to weigh before saucing — there was no point in the process where
a clean number existed. And the 5.7 lbs raw was package weight recorded before
trimming, so it includes meat that never entered the crockpot. Backing the
sauce out by its ingredients gives an apparent 0.565, but that is an estimate
subtracted from an estimate and it falls below every crockpot figure on file.
Not recorded.

v2 adds a weigh step between shredding and saucing, raises the buy quantity to
5 ¾ lbs to account for trim that package weight hides, and redefines
`per_container.chicken` as sauced weight since that is what can actually be
put on a scale.

Frozen broccoli measured 0.931 — 1020 g in, 950 g steamed. Against 0.38–0.48
for fresh crowns that is dramatically more predictable, which is a second
argument for frozen beyond the labour saved. A fourth bag was bought as
insurance and was not needed; 95 g per container against a 100 g target is
not worth opening it.

Rice at 451 g per dry cup. The three 3 ½-cup sessions now read 451, 455 and
457, which is tight enough to plan on. The outlying 407 came from the single
3-cup session, suggesting that reading scaled oddly rather than the yield
being genuinely variable.
