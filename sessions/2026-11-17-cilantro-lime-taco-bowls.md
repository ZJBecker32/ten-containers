## What happened

Off-spec cook, and deliberately recorded as one. Five low-sodium packets went
into the meat instead of three, on top of the bulk blend that stands in for a
fourth. One plain, unseasoned pound of 90/10 beef was cooked and folded in, and
the batch was stretched to **12 containers** to bring sodium back under the
ceiling. The rice went up to 4 ¼ cups to keep pace.

Protein was 4.8 lbs of ground turkey plus that 1 lb of beef.

**Sodium is roughly 775 mg per container across 12.** Each extra low-sodium
packet is ~104 mg per container at 10, so 10 containers would have landed near
930. This was arithmetic, not a measurement. It also means this cook cannot
confirm v4's 700 mg projection, which was the open sodium question on the
recipe. That stays unverified.

**The meat number is not a yield.** 2500 g off 2631 g raw is 0.95, which no
ground meat does; the packet water and five packets of seasoning are in that
weight, and two proteins with different yields are mixed. Same contamination
as buffalo's first cook. Not recorded as a yield.

**The vegetable question is closed.** It had been open since July: the recipe
claimed 110 g per container, while the measured pepper and onion figures only
supported 76–87 g. This cook measured the combined veg directly at **1015 g**,
which is ~100 g per container at the standard 10. The claim was about 10% high,
not 30%. The rest of the gap was onion: this cook used 2 medium onions where
the recipe said 1 large, and the old arithmetic had priced that large onion at
the ~80 g of a medium one.

Beans: 3 cans to 745 g rinsed and warmed, about 248 g per can.

Rice at 476 g per dry cup, but at 4 ¼ cups, so not comparable to the
3 ½-cup readings of 451–461. Every session so far has given more rice per cup
the bigger the batch: 407 at 3 cups, ~456 at 3 ½, 476 at 4 ¼.

Portioned 12 × 208 g seasoned meat, 169 g rice, 62 g beans, 85 g veg.
Roughly 710 kcal and 55 g of protein per container, all estimated.
