## What happened

First cook of this recipe in any form, after four versions on paper. Two
deliberate deviations from v5: fresh broccoli instead of frozen, to test the
new air fryer, and 3 ½ cups of rice instead of the 3 ¼ the recipe specifies.

**The chicken is a new low for the crockpot — 0.539 on package weight.** The
three sessions on file now read 0.637, 0.572 and 0.539 in date order, which is
a steady march downward rather than scatter. The likely cause is compliance
with this repo's own guidance: each cook has trimmed more deliberately and
carried the cook further toward shreddability, and both of those remove weight
from a package basis. 0.54 may not be the floor.

The overshoot cancelled it out almost exactly. 6.06 lbs bought against a
5 ¾ lb spec returned 1481 g of meat, where 5 ¾ lbs at the old 0.57 planning
figure predicted 1487 g. Buying 5% long absorbed a yield that came in 5% short.

**The sauce weighed 520 g**, the first clean sauce measurement anywhere in this
repo — buffalo had to estimate its own at 454 g and never confirmed it. That
makes the sauced-versus-unsauced convention a measured quantity here rather
than an inference: 200 g per container of which 148 g is meat and 52 g is
sauce. Projection was ~450 g, so the real sauce ran 16% heavier.

**Fresh broccoli yielded 0.399.** Against the three crown sessions now on file
— 0.481, 0.377, 0.399 — that is unremarkable, sitting just above the bottom.
It is emphatically not the 0.931 of frozen florets. 7.49 lbs of crowns
produced 1356 g where 4 bags of frozen would have produced ~1266 g for a
fraction of the work and cost.

The appliance split was not recorded, so this figure cannot be attributed to
the air fryer specifically. If the load went across both the Crispi and the
toaster oven, 0.399 is a blend of the two and the head-to-head comparison did
not happen. Worth knowing before this gets treated as an air fryer number.

Rice at 461 g per dry cup. Four readings at 3 ½ cups now read 451, 455, 457,
461 — mean 456, and every one above the 450 planning figure.

Portioned 200 g sauced chicken, 161 g rice, 136 g broccoli — a 497 g container
against v5's planned 462 g, almost all of it the extra rice and broccoli.
Recalculates to ~554 kcal and 53 g of protein.
