## What happened

Beef bought as four packages of roughly 1.4 lbs — 1.43 + 1.39 + 1.45 + 1.41 =
5.68 lbs, over the 5 lbs the recipe called for, because Aldi does not sell
lean ground beef in a size that adds to 5. Four packages is the realistic
purchase and the recipe now says so.

Rice was portioned into 10 before the beef finished, which fixed the container
count at 10. At 3 ½ cups it came out at 159 g per container against a 140–150
target, so the recipe drops to 3 ¼ cups.

Broccoli came up short again: 966 g against a 1200 g plan, 96 g per container.
Second measurement of broccoli crowns, and the two disagree sharply —
5.68 lbs → 1240 g (0.481) and 5.65 lbs → 966 g (0.377) on near-identical raw
weight.

Both were weighed the same way, package weight of crowns before trimming, and
both were air roasted in the Ninja in two batches. So this is not a
measurement-basis problem. It is the product: crowns vary in how much stalk
comes attached, and stalk is dense and mostly gets trimmed off. Nothing about
the process changes it.

Deliberately not adopting a trim-then-weigh protocol. It would tighten the
number and change no decision — 5 ½ lbs of crowns lands between 94 and 120 g
per container across the full observed range, and the standing target is
90–120. The extra weighing would buy precision nobody acts on.
